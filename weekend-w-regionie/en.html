Weekend w regionie: museums, castles, nature and forest camping in Poland

Free weekend and no plan? The app shows what is worth seeing nearby: museums, castles, national parks, nature reserves, forest nature trails, swimming areas and places where you can legally spend the night in the forest. Works offline, with no account and no ads.

See what it does
What you’ll find

Over 7,300 places from open public data

The data comes from Polish public registers, the Polish State Forests, the European Environment Agency and OpenStreetMap. It is built into the app, and the app downloads newer data on its own.

A list and a map around you

Places sorted by distance, within 5 to 200 km. You can also search around any town – handy for planning a trip.

Forest camping

The “Only places with forest camping” filter shows places in the “Zanocuj w lesie” program – “Stay overnight in the forest” – of the Polish State Forests, where you can legally spend the night in the forest.

Parks, reserves, trails

Boundaries and routes on the map, with parking. Pick a parking lot and navigate straight to it.

Places you’ve visited

“I’m here” logs a place with one tap. Forgot to log a visit? Add it later from a photo with a saved location.

Local flavors

Over 2,100 traditional products from every Polish region, worth trying while you are there.

Private

No account and no ads. Your location, favorites and visits stay on your phone only.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does the app cost?

It’s free, with no ads and no account.

Is the app in English?

Yes. The app is in English on phones set to any language other than Polish, and you can switch the language in the “More” tab. Place names, addresses and descriptions from the Polish public registers stay in Polish, as in the official data.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All the data is inside the app, so the list and the place cards work even with no signal. The map uses Google Maps, so the map background needs an internet connection.

Where does my location go?

Nowhere. It is used only on your phone, to sort places by distance and to log visits. Details are in the privacy policy.

Where does the data come from?

From open public data: dane.gov.pl, the General Directorate for Environmental Protection, the Polish Geological Institute, the National Heritage Board of Poland, the Polish State Forests, the European Environment Agency and OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). The full list is in the app, in the “More” tab.

Is this an official government app?

No. Weekend w regionie is an independent app. It is not affiliated with any government office or public institution. Before you go, check the opening hours on the place’s website.

I found an error in the data. What should I do?

Write to kontakt@appstorm.eu. The data in the app is updated every week.
